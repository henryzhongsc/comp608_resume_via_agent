# Amazon Robotics — Data Scientist (New Grad)

## DESCRIPTION

Are you inspired by invention? Is problem solving through teamwork in your DNA?

Amazon Robotics seeks talented professionals to apply cutting-edge advances in
robotics and software to solve real-world challenges. The team automates
fulfillment center operations using autonomous mobile robots, control software,
computer vision, machine learning, and related technologies, with dedicated
focus on research and development.

## Key job responsibilities

- Design and implement state-of-the-art solutions for novel problems
- Collaborate with research and robotics experts to design experiments and
  research algorithms
- Partner with technology and product leaders on scientific problem-solving
  approaches
- Build tools and generate business insights for customers
- Quantify system performance at scale and expand analytical breadth
- Evolve key performance indicator libraries and construct efficient experiments
- Engage with software development and warehouse design teams

## A day in the life

As part of the Software Research and Data Science (SWRDS) team, members may
conduct deep analysis of robotics field data, focus on modeling and pipelines,
or work on early-concept solution analysis using simulations and ML-based
models.

## About the team

The Amazon Robotics Software Research and Data Science (SWRDS) team builds and
runs simulation experiments to understand system performance, including
operational scaling, bottlenecks, and robustness testing that informs critical
engineering decisions.

## BASIC QUALIFICATIONS

- Master's degree in quantitative field (statistics, mathematics, data science,
  business analytics, economics, finance, engineering, computer science) OR
  Bachelor's degree with 3+ years professional/military experience
- 1+ year relevant academic research or industry experience in Machine Learning,
  Applied Statistics, Computer Vision, Optimization, or related field
- 1+ year experience with data querying languages (SQL), scripting languages
  (Python), or statistical/mathematical software (R, SAS, Matlab)

## PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS

- 1+ years as data scientist or similar role involving data extraction,
  analysis, statistical modeling, and communication
- 2+ years as data/research scientist, statistician, or quantitative analyst in
  internet-based company with complex, big data sources
