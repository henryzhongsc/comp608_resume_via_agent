# Software Development Engineer - 2026 (US)

## DESCRIPTION

Do you want to solve real customer problems through innovative technology? Do
you enjoy working on scalable services in a collaborative team environment?

Amazon seeks SDEs to work on meaningful challenges impacting millions globally.
The role emphasizes ownership throughout the code lifecycle, from design through
deployment and operations. The position offers opportunities for professional
growth in a culture supporting learning and mentorship, with development cycles
measured in weeks rather than years.

## Key job responsibilities

- Collaborate with cross-disciplinary teams to design, build, and operate
  innovative products
- Design and develop scalable solutions using cloud-native architectures and
  microservices
- Participate in code reviews and contribute technical documentation
- Build and maintain resilient distributed systems that are scalable and
  fault-tolerant
- Leverage and contribute to the development of GenAI and AI-powered tools
- Write clean, maintainable code following best practices
- Work in agile environments practicing CI/CD principles with on-call
  responsibilities
- Demonstrate operational excellence through monitoring and resolving production
  issues

## BASIC QUALIFICATIONS

- Experience with programming languages: Java, Python, C++, C#, Go, Rust, or
  TypeScript
- Data structure implementation, algorithm development, or object-oriented
  design experience
- Currently has, or is in the process of obtaining a bachelor's degree in
  Computer Science, Computer Engineering, Data Science, Information Systems, or
  related STEM fields
- Must be 18 years or older

## PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS

- Previous technical internship or demonstrated project experience
- Experience with AI tools, AWS, database systems (SQL/NoSQL), open-source
  contributions, version control, or debugging complex systems
- Quick learning and technology adaptation capability
- Basic understanding of software development lifecycle (SDLC)
- Strong problem-solving and analytical skills
- Excellent written and verbal communication skills
